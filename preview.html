Sources, scope, and checks

Lesson references come from 1662 for SingTheOffice.xlsx: 366 daily entries and 90 named entries. Prayer additions come from Additions to the BCP 1662.docx. The original bodies and source locations are retained in the data file.

No feast-transfer policy is silently supplied. Coinciding appointments require a choice. Null lesson cells remain visible when no fallback exists. The St. Stephen reading discrepancy is flagged, not corrected.

Gregorian dates are used. For the daily Ember prayer, an Ember Week is implemented as Sunday through Saturday containing the appointed Ember Wednesday. This boundary convention is documented separately from the Prayer Book's instruction to pray every day in those weeks.

The seasonal label is informational, not a Collect selector. Communion readings, carry-over collects, evening anticipations, complete service texts, and final liturgical verification remain for the service-building stage.
