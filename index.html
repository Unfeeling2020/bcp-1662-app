Sources, scope, and checks

What this page does

This is the service renderer, not the earlier preview. It assembles Morning Prayer, Evening Prayer, the Communion ordinary, and Ante-Communion from the selected date. It uses the original-1662 lesson table imported from 1662 for SingTheOffice.xlsx and the supplied Additions to the BCP 1662, without silently repairing their wording.

Reading preferences

Under Service settings, select one or more of the eleven opening sentences, separately for Morning and Evening Prayer. Choices appear in Prayer Book order. Special-petition clauses are enabled by default and printed in italics when included; the checkbox remains optional. Line spacing defaults to Single / compact, with the previous Comfortable spacing available. These preferences are saved in this browser. Existing choices from version 0.2.2 are retained. Communion and Ante-Communion each have a separate Offertory sentence picker, with all twenty choices available. New Offertory selections start with the first sentence; later choices are remembered.

Readings and textual limits

Canonical Office lessons are loaded as KJV from Arul John's Bible-kjv repository. A KJV source link remains available on failure. This is a modern-spelling KJV transcription, not a diplomatic transcription of a 1611 printing. Apocryphal Office lessons also load inline as Authorized / KJV text, from the machine-readable KJV-with-Apocrypha source, whose transcription derives from eBible. This is not a word-for-word import of the originally linked website. Your specified Apocrypha source remains linked for reference or when a request fails. Sirach/Ecclesiasticus, Susanna, and Bel and the Dragon are mapped explicitly. Baruch 6 (the Epistle of Jeremy) is built into the page because the chapter source lists only Baruch 1-5; its 73 verses were checked against eBible KJV Baruch 6. Compared with the originally linked chapter, its evident transcription errors at verses 5, 59, 71 and 72 are corrected (afraid of them; a palace; cast; them). Other chapters require an internet connection; successful chapter requests are reused during the current visit, and failed requests can be retried. Communion Epistles and Gospels are embedded in this page for all ninety propers already supported by its calendar and proper selector. Their wording follows the printed BCP propers, not generic KJV extracts; opening capitalization and paragraph breaks are normalized. A collapsible source reference remains available beneath each passage. These readings and all twenty Offertory sentences need no separate network request after the page loads. The Office KJV checkbox affects Office lessons only. The two advance-warning exhortations and the full administrative rubrics remain available through source links rather than all reproduced in the service stream.

The Psalms are extracted as text from the existing local bcp.js. Its abbreviated service texts are not used or executed. This preserves that file's transcription; it is not a fresh collation of every Psalm against a printed edition. Common prayers and collects are transcribed from the public-domain BCP text; capitalization and punctuation are not a facsimile. A complete independent textual and rubrical audit remains outstanding.

Ash Wednesday

Morning Prayer automatically includes the full Commination after the completed Litany, including its ordinary closing prayers. It is built into this page and is not appended to Evening Prayer or either Communion service. Its Service settings checkbox is enabled by default. While enabled on Ash Wednesday, the Litany is included as a prerequisite without changing the saved Litany preference for other days. Unchecking it returns to that preference. Six evident transcription corrections, checked against the Church of England text, are listed in the Commination source disclosure. No other source wording or modern replacement rite has been substituted.

Calendar conventions

Modern Gregorian civil dates are used. Coinciding appointments require an explicit choice; feasts are not silently transferred. The Office lesson table remains separate from the Communion propers. The collect is anticipated at Evening Prayer before Sundays and feasts with vigils; fasting-day transfers are not used to move the lesson table. Ember weeks use the documented Sunday-through-Saturday convention. Extra Trinity Sundays require a minister's choice of an omitted Epiphany proper; the Sunday next before Advent uses the final Trinity proper.

The source's St. Stephen reading, Ecclesiastes 4, remains flagged rather than silently changed to Ecclesiasticus 4. American state adaptations and the additional intentions are the project owner's directions, not a claim to reproduce an unmodified Church of England edition. Presidential forms come from the supplied document. January 20 is an annual intention, moved to January 21 only when January 20 is Sunday. It does not duplicate a presidential prayer.
