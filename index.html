Sources, scope, and checks

What this page does

This is the service renderer, not the earlier preview. It assembles Morning Prayer, Evening Prayer, the Communion ordinary, and Ante-Communion from the selected date. It uses the original-1662 lesson table imported from 1662 for SingTheOffice.xlsx and the supplied Additions to the BCP 1662, without silently repairing their wording.

Readings and textual limits

Canonical Office lessons are loaded as KJV from Arul John's Bible-kjv repository. A KJV source link remains available on failure. This is a modern-spelling KJV transcription, not a diplomatic transcription of a 1611 printing. Apocryphal lessons open in the specified Apocrypha source. Communion Epistles and Gospels open in the corresponding printed BCP propers; they are not yet embedded. The two advance-warning exhortations, alternative Offertory sentences and the full administrative rubrics are available through source links rather than all reproduced in the service stream.

The Psalms are extracted as text from the existing local bcp.js. Its abbreviated service texts are not used or executed. This preserves that file's transcription; it is not a fresh collation of every Psalm against a printed edition. Common prayers and collects are transcribed from the public-domain BCP text; capitalization and punctuation are not a facsimile. A complete independent textual and rubrical audit remains outstanding.

Calendar conventions

Modern Gregorian civil dates are used. Coinciding appointments require an explicit choice; feasts are not silently transferred. The Office lesson table remains separate from the Communion propers. The collect is anticipated at Evening Prayer before Sundays and feasts with vigils; fasting-day transfers are not used to move the lesson table. Ember weeks use the documented Sunday-through-Saturday convention. Extra Trinity Sundays require a minister's choice of an omitted Epiphany proper; the Sunday next before Advent uses the final Trinity proper.

The source's St. Stephen reading, Ecclesiastes 4, remains flagged rather than silently changed to Ecclesiasticus 4. American state adaptations and the additional intentions are the project owner's directions, not a claim to reproduce an unmodified Church of England edition. Presidential forms come from the supplied document. January 20 is an annual intention, moved to January 21 only when January 20 is Sunday. It does not duplicate a presidential prayer.
